How the forecast works

A statistical model trained on recent hourly Central readings projects the next 24 hours from current levels, the last day's average and the daily cycle. It is then nudged by wind direction, forecast showers and ASMC hotspot counts. Treat it as a guide; NEA's own advisories take precedence.

Sources: NEA via data.gov.sg (PM2.5, PSI, 24-hour forecast) and ASEAN Specialised Meteorological Centre (hotspots, alert level). Official advisories: haze.gov.sg.
